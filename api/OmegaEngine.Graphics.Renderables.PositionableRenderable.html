ICollection<PositionableRenderable>

Remarks

A renderable lives in exactly one collection at a time: either this or Positionables. Adding it here removes it from its previous collection and keeps its local Position, Rotation, Scale and PreTransform, i.e. its world position changes.

Setting Visible to false hides the entire subtree. A View skips the entire subtree if SubtreeBoundingSphere or SubtreeBoundingBox is outside its view frustum.

Will be disposed when Dispose() is called.

FloatingTransformCached

Transforms from entity space into floating space, with minimum screen size scaling and billboarding but without distance compression.

protected Matrix FloatingTransformCached { get; }

Property Value

Matrix

Remarks

The transform behind FloatingBoundingSphere and FloatingBoundingBox.

Distance compression scales uniformly around the camera, which leaves the view frustum, the apparent size and any ray starting at the camera unchanged. So this suffices for culling and picking against what is rendered, while keeping bodies at their true distances for lighting and shadows.

Cached value, without ensuring it is up-to-date.

MinScreenSizeDistance

The renderable never appears smaller on screen than it does at this distance from the Camera, keeping distant objects visible.

public float? MinScreenSizeDistance { get; set; }

Property Value

float?

Remarks

While closer than this distance the renderable renders at its natural size; farther away it is scaled up to preserve its apparent size (angular diameter) at this distance.

The scaling is applied on top of Scale and is reflected in the bounding bodies used for culling. Combine with DistanceCompressionStart for very large, very distant objects.

PhysicalTransform
